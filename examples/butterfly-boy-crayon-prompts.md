## fig1 · 发光的狗 → good dog（奶黄底）

EN scene: A big shaggy black dog lying down with its chin on its paws, gazing up gently, its fur rendered in layered deep teal and navy crayon strokes with tiny warm amber dash accents; a small orange ball beside its paw; flat warm cream background; two hand-drawn sparkles near the dog. Hand-lettered playful lowercase caption: "good dog"

CN: 一只毛茸茸的大黑狗趴着、下巴搁在爪子上，温柔地抬头看；毛用深青与藏蓝蜡笔笔触叠色，缀暖琥珀色短划；爪子旁一只橙色小球；奶黄色平涂背景；狗旁边两个手绘星形闪光。手写小字："good dog"。

## fig2 · 宋庄二十平米 → my tiny studio（薄荷绿底）

EN scene: A small wooden easel holding a half-finished canvas with a wobbly colorful abstract painting, a dented tin bucket crammed with paintbrushes leaning against its leg, rendered in warm brown and ochre crayon strokes with tiny teal dash accents; flat pale mint-green background; two hand-drawn sparkles. Hand-lettered playful lowercase caption: "my tiny studio"

CN: 一个小木画架，上面立着一幅画了一半、歪歪扭扭的彩色抽象画；腿边靠着一只凹陷的铁皮桶，塞满画笔；暖棕与赭石蜡笔笔触，缀青色短划；薄荷绿平涂背景；两个手绘星形闪光。手写小字："my tiny studio"。

## fig3 · 蝴蝶少年 → butterfly boy（宝蓝底）

EN scene: An open cupped child's hand seen from the side, palm up, with a single glowing golden butterfly hovering just above the fingertips, tiny golden dots drifting around it; hand rendered in soft peach and pink crayon strokes, butterfly in bright yellow and orange; flat deep royal blue background; a few hand-drawn sparkles. Hand-lettered playful lowercase caption: "butterfly boy"

CN: 一只侧看的摊开小手，掌心向上，一只发光的金色蝴蝶悬在指尖上方，金色小光点飘散；手用柔桃色与粉色蜡笔笔触，蝴蝶用明黄与橙色；深宝蓝平涂背景；几个手绘星形闪光。手写小字："butterfly boy"。

## fig4 · 下凡 → draw with me（暖粉底）

EN scene: A sheet of white paper with a charmingly clumsy child's crayon drawing of a smiling dog on it, three fat crayons in red, blue and yellow lying beside the paper, all rendered in waxy crayon strokes; flat warm peachy-pink background; two hand-drawn starbursts. Hand-lettered playful lowercase caption: "draw with me"

CN: 一张白纸，上面是孩子画的憨拙可爱的微笑小狗蜡笔画；纸旁躺着红、蓝、黄三支胖蜡笔；全部用蜡质蜡笔笔触表现；暖桃粉平涂背景；两个手绘星爆符号。手写小字："draw with me"。
