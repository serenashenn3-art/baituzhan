**角色锚定（全系列一致）**：
EN: A slim Chinese painter in his mid-twenties, short slightly messy black hair, wearing a dark blue long-sleeve shirt
CN: 向帅：清瘦、二十六七岁、短而微乱的黑发、藏蓝色长袖上衣

**统一光线策略**：冷蓝紫环境 + 一处明确的暖橙光源（双色调打光，本风格核心）——

## fig1 · 发光的狗（画室与皮皮）

EN scene: Inside a cramped art studio at night, the painter sits on a small stool holding a canvas on his knees, gazing at a big black dog lying beside him whose fur softly glows warm amber; a single desk lamp casts warm orange light over man and dog while the cluttered room around them sinks into cool violet-blue; easels, paint tubes, brushes in tins and stacked canvases fill every corner, all fully drawn

CN: 深夜拥挤的画室里，向帅坐在小凳上、膝盖上架着画布，凝视趴在脚边的大黑狗皮皮，狗毛发出柔和的琥珀色微光；一盏台灯把暖橙光打在一人一狗身上，四周杂物沉进冷蓝紫里；画架、颜料管、插满笔的铁罐、摞起的画布挤满每个角落，全部完整刻画。

## fig2 · 宋庄二十平米

EN scene: A tiny twenty-square-meter rented room in Songzhuang artist village at night: an iron-frame bed with a thin blanket, a wooden easel holding an unfinished canvas, a dented metal bucket full of brushes, paint tubes and sketches stacked on every surface; the painter sits on the bed edge pulling on a jacket; a small orange electric heater glows warm on the floor, its light catching man and easel while the rest of the cramped room falls into cold violet-blue; every object fully drawn, dense clutter everywhere

CN: 宋庄画家村二十平米的出租屋深夜：铁架床上搭着薄被，木画架上立着未完成的画，凹陷的铁桶插满画笔，颜料管和速写堆满每个平面；向帅坐在床沿披外套；地上小电暖器发出暖橙光，照亮人和画架，屋子其余部分沉进冷蓝紫；杂物密集，全部完整刻画。

## fig3 · 蝴蝶少年

EN scene: On a rooftop at night the painter stands with one open palm raised, a single butterfly glowing warm golden light resting above his fingertips, its glow illuminating his upturned face and hand; the dark city skyline and deep blue night sky behind him rendered in cool violet-blue with densely drawn rooftops, antennas and wires; a few faint unlit butterflies drift in the darkness around, all fully drawn

CN: 夜晚的天台上，向帅站立着摊开一只手掌，一只发出暖金色光的蝴蝶悬停在他指尖上方，蝶光照亮他仰起的脸和手；身后是冷蓝紫的城市天际线与深蓝夜空，屋顶、天线和电线密集刻画；黑暗中还有几只未发光的蝴蝶在四周飘浮，全部完整刻画。

## fig4 · 下凡（教孩子画画）

EN scene: In a village classroom in the early evening the painter crouches beside a wooden desk guiding a small child's hand holding a crayon over a drawing of a dog; three other children lean in watching; a warm hanging bulb casts orange light over the little group while the classroom around them with its blackboard, worn desks and paper drawings pinned on the wall sinks into cool violet-blue, every detail fully drawn

CN: 乡村教室的傍晚，向帅蹲在木课桌旁，握着一个小孩拿蜡笔的手在一张画着狗的纸上引导；另外三个孩子凑近观看；一盏暖色吊灯把橙光打在这一小群人身上，教室四周的黑板、旧课桌、墙上钉着的画沉进冷蓝紫，每个细节完整刻画。
